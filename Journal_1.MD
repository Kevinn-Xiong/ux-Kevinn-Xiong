Not too long ago, I visited my friend and wanted to use their microwave to heat up some food. I figured that it would be a very simple task as I have used a microwave many times before. 
But my friend's microwave was different from what I was used to. 

![Microwave full](./microwave%20full.jpg)


The very first thing that I tried to look for was the numbers to enter the time. After noticing that the microwave doesn't have them, I saw a big knob that turns with a start/quick start button in the middle. Above that was the other options like defrosting, timer, and etc. I wasn't too sure how to use the microwave at first. I was used to using microwaves with a number pad so my mental model or my understanding of how something works based on experience, didn't help with this microwave. My initial thinking was that I had to turn the knob and press the start button to get it working.
